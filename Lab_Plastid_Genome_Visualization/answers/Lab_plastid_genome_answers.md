**1.** The plant is *Bougainvillea peruviana*. The accession is NC_049005.1.

**2.** The genome is 154,465 bp long.

**3.** Yes. The LSC is the biggest part, at positions 1 to 85,563. IRA follows it (85,564 to 110,989). Then comes the SSC (110,989 to 129,039), and IRB ends the circle (129,040 to 154,465). On the map they are labeled <check these labels on your image>.

**4.** Three genes in the LSC are psbA, matK and rbcL.

**5.** Genes in the SSC include rpl32, ccsA and trnL-UAG.

**6.** The gene rpl2 is in an inverted repeat. It is shown twice because the IRA and IRB are copies of the same sequence, so each gene in them is drawn two times. The rRNA genes and ycf2 are also duplicated.

**7.** psbA makes the D1 protein of photosystem II, which is part of the reaction center that captures light energy during photosynthesis.

**8.** The arrows show which strand a gene is read from. Genes drawn on the outside of the circle are transcribed one way, and genes on the inside are transcribed the other way. So transcription happens on both strands of the plastid genome, not just one.
